# Scenario 5: Year-1 Only Ambassadors

**Description**: Ambassador program active for first 12 months only, then discontinued for new referrals.

### How It Works

- **Year 1**: Full ambassador program (15%/5% commissions)
- **Year 2+**: No new ambassador signups, existing ambassadors keep earning on their referrals
- **Blended model**: Revenue improves as user base grows beyond Year 1 cohort

### Net Profit Per User (Blended Over Time)

| Period | Premium Net | Emerging Net | Average |
|--------|-------------|--------------|---------|
| Year 1 (with ambassadors) | $3.66 | $0.66 | $2.16 |
| Year 2+ (no ambassadors) | $5.26 | $1.06 | $3.16 |
| Year 3 blend (70% new) | $4.78 | $0.94 | $2.86 |
| Year 5 blend (85% new) | $5.02 | $1.00 | $3.01 |

### Projected Revenue (100K users, blended)

| Year | Ambassador Cohort | New Cohort | Blended Net/User | Monthly Net |
|------|-------------------|------------|------------------|-------------|
| Year 1 | 100% | 0% | $2.16 | $16,640 |
| Year 2 | 50% | 50% | $2.66 | $19,728 |
| Year 3 | 30% | 70% | $2.86 | $21,424 |
| Year 5 | 15% | 85% | $3.01 | $22,548 |

### Annual Revenue Summary (at 100K users)

| Year | Annual | vs Scenario 1 |
|------|--------|---------------|
| Year 1 | $200K | — |
| Year 2 | $237K | +19% |
| Year 3 | $257K | +29% |
| Year 5 | $271K | +35% |

### Trade-off Analysis

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Year 1 Growth | Maximized via ambassadors |
| Long-term Margin | Improves each year |

# Appendix: Calculation Reference

### Subscription Net Profit Formula

```
Net Profit = Price - Platform Fee - AI Cost - Ambassador Commissions

Where:
- Platform Fee = Price × 0.30 (mobile) or Price × 0.029 + $0.30 (Stripe)
- AI Cost = $0.33 (heavy user assumption)
- Tier 1 Commission = Price × 0.15
- Tier 2 Commission = Price × 0.05
```

### Free User Cost Formula

```
Free User Cost = Users × 0.96 × $0.004

Where:
- 0.96 = percentage who are free (at 4% conversion)
- $0.004 = average AI cost per free user per month
```

### Ad Revenue Formula

```
Monthly Ad Revenue = Emerging Free Users × (eCPM / 1000) × Impressions/User

Where:
- Emerging Free Users = Total Users × 0.48 × 0.96
- Impressions/User = Sessions × Ads per Session
```

### Credit Pack Revenue Formula

```
Pack Revenue = Pro Users × Buyer % × Avg Pack Value × Purchase Frequency
```

### Church License Revenue Formula

```
Church Revenue = (Total Users / 10,000) × Churches per 10K × Avg License Value
```
